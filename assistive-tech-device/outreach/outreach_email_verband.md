## English (reference only — send the German version above)

**Subject:** School project: obstacle-detection wearable — seeking
feedback from real users

Dear Sir or Madam,

My name is [YOUR NAME], I'm [YOUR AGE] years old, and I'm building a
wearable obstacle-detection device as a school/hobby project: a wristband
with a forward-facing distance sensor that vibrates to indicate how close
an obstacle is.

I want to be upfront about where this actually stands: it's an early
prototype that I've only tested myself so far (blindfolded, supervised).
I deliberately do **not** want to present this as a finished solution for
blind or low-vision people before it's gotten real feedback from someone
who actually navigates with a visual impairment. That's exactly why I'm
writing to you.

My question: do you know anyone in your organization who'd be willing to

- talk briefly about the idea and concept now (e.g. by phone or video
  call), or
- — once a working prototype exists — try the device in a supervised
  test setting and give honest feedback (critical feedback especially
  welcome)?

There's no time pressure and no obligation. It matters to me and my
parents that this is handled seriously and with everyone's informed
agreement — a parent is involved in this outreach and would accompany any
further in-person meeting.

I'd really appreciate any response, including just follow-up questions.
