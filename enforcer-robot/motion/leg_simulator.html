Leg motion prototype

One of Sesame's 2-DOF legs, side view. The left leg goes straight to the target the way naive servo code does. The right leg uses the motion engine. Same target, same duration — the difference is entirely in how it gets there. Get the feel right here, then port the curve to the ESP32.
